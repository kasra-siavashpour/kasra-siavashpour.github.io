Research

  • FAIR: envy-free routing for multi-agent traffic

    A routing model in which no vehicle envies another's share of the delay, with a guarantee that holds at every step of a trip. I built the evaluation simulator, which runs on 7 city road networks with up to 132K trips each. When it contradicted two of our lemmas, we revised them.

  • The Loop of the Rings: decentralized cooperation

    A fully decentralized cooperative system built from randomized rings of participants; with at least 60% honest participants, everyone who acts correctly gets their service or payment. I contributed to the worst-case analysis, implemented the protocol core in Go, and ran the simulations that test the theory. Preliminary version on arXiv

  • A survey of self-organizing cellular networks

    A survey of how 5G and 6G networks configure, optimize, heal and protect themselves. I wrote about half of the survey and reviewed about half of its 114 papers.

Earlier, I worked on nature-inspired cluster routing for FANET and IoT networks with Dr. Bardia Safaei, and on WebAssembly for systems acceleration with Dr. Gianni Antichi (Politecnico di Milano).
